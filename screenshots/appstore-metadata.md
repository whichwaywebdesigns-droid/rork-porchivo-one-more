## Description

```
Porchivo is the only app that calculates a real-time porch risk score for every incoming package — before theft happens.

PORCH RISK SCORE
Every delivery gets a 0–100 risk score factoring neighborhood theft alerts, delivery timing, and your Theft Shield status. When risk hits 65+, you get an immediate alert.

TRACK ANY PACKAGE
Follow shipments across 1,400+ carriers with live status updates. Premium members get 90-second refresh so nothing slips through.

NEIGHBORHOOD ALERTS
Report and view theft activity on a live neighborhood map. Every alert your neighbors file feeds directly into the risk score for your block.

HOA & COMMUNITY MODE
Built for HOAs, condos, and multifamily buildings. Staff and board members get a full operations suite:
• Package operations board — log, track, and close out deliveries for all residents
• Announcements — scheduled posts, priority levels, and a template library
• Incident queue — file, triage, escalate, and resolve package incidents with SLA tracking
• Community calendar — HOA meetings, amenity bookings, maintenance windows, and RSVPs
• Maintenance requests — residents submit work orders; staff manages the queue
• Admin dashboard — community health score, analytics, and a full audit log

PLANS
Start free. Premium unlocks faster tracking, advanced risk scoring, and priority alerts. Family and HOA plans cover up to 5 members or whole communities. Annual plans include a 7-day free trial.

Stop guessing whether your package is safe. Porchivo tells you — before it's too late.
```
