Under the hood

Three signals. One score. Zero guesswork.

Porchivo continuously weighs the factors that actually predict porch theft — no cameras, no sensors, no guesswork required.

Timing patterns

When the package lands matters. Deliveries arriving during high-theft windows — mid-day, when porches sit empty — score higher automatically.

Neighborhood activity

Live theft activity around your community feeds the score. A quiet week lowers it; a spike nearby raises it — before the next truck arrives.

Your delivery history

Your community's own patterns refine every score. Congestion, past incidents, and handoff trends make predictions sharper over time.
