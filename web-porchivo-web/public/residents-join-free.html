How residents join your community

Your management team distributes one invite code through your existing email or resident portal. That's the entire rollout plan.

1. Download the app

Residents grab Porchivo from the App Store or Google Play — on the phone they already have. Nothing to install at the property, nothing to configure.

2. Enter the invite code

One code ties them to your community. They're in, verified, and receiving package protection — in about a minute.

3. Get protected instantly

Risk scoring, theft alerts, and Porch Partner handoffs activate immediately. Residents can opt into the Partner network whenever they want.

Optional — always opt-in

Residents can become Porch Partners

Porch Partners are trusted neighbors who opt in to receive and hold deliveries for others when risk is high or no one's home. Partners earn extra income per secure handoff and build a neighborhood reputation score.

It's opt-in only — no resident is ever obligated, and your board controls whether the network is enabled for your community.
